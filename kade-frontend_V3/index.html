Questions shop owners ask

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new shop gets 14 days free with Starter features. No card is needed. Before the trial ends, pick a plan to keep your store open. Everything you set up is kept.

How do I pay for my plan?

By card (Visa, Mastercard or Amex through OnePay) or by bank transfer. Card payments activate your plan straight away. Bank transfers are activated after we check your slip.

Can my customers pay by card?

Yes, through your own OnePay merchant account, so the money goes straight to your bank. You also need your refund, return, privacy and terms pages and your contact details on your store. Sidadiya creates these for you to review. Cash on delivery and bank transfer work from day one.

What if I miss a month?

You get reminders first. After the plan ends your store stays open for a short grace period, then it pauses. Your products, photos and orders are kept, and everything returns when you renew.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They open your store link in their phone browser, the same way they open any website.

Can I use my own web address?

Not yet. Your store lives at www.sidadiya.com/store/your-shop. Custom domains are on our roadmap.
