Maintenance mode

Close Sidadiya for everyone except admins while you update it.

Closes Sidadiya for everyone except admins. Stores, dashboards and checkout show an animated "shop is closed" page and reopen by themselves when you switch this off. Payment callbacks and emails keep working.

Buyer notice

The popup buyers see when they open any store.

A popup buyers see when they open any store, explaining that each shop is responsible for its own products, delivery and refunds. After they tap the button it stays hidden on their device for 30 days, or until you change the text.
