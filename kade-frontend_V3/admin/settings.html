Plans and features

Prices are LKR per month. A struck-through price is optional and must be higher than the price. Feature switches decide what each plan unlocks, immediately, for every shop on that plan. Leave a limit empty for unlimited.

Free trial

New shops only. Reminders go out 4 days and 1 day before the end.

Payment settings: OnePay

Sidadiya's own OnePay account, used when shops pay for their plan by card. Keys are stored encrypted on the server and are never shown again after saving.

Sidadiya contact details

Shown in the main site footer and on /legal/contact. Card networks require an email, a physical address (not a P.O. Box) and a phone number.

Sidadiya legal pages

The platform's own Refund, Return, Privacy and Terms pages. Clear a box to go back to the default template. Use "## " for headings and "- " for bullets. Have these reviewed by a lawyer before launch.

“How to get OnePay” guide

Shown to every seller in Store settings > Payment gateways. Lists take one item per line. Check fees and documents with OnePay from time to time.

Marketing

Social proof on the main Sidadiya page, shown as “40+ shops onboarded”. Set the count to 0 to hide it.
