## How to boost them (simple plan)

1. **Order:** start with Post 1 (the widest message). Add Posts 2, 3 and 5 two or three days later. Post 4 works best once people already know Sidadiya.
2. **Goal:** in Ads Manager choose **Traffic** (link clicks to the website). Use the link given for each post.
3. **Audience:** Sri Lanka, age 20 to 50. Interests such as Small business, Entrepreneurship, Online shopping, Home business and Facebook Marketplace. Placements: Facebook and Instagram feeds.
4. **Budget to test:** about Rs. 1,000 to 1,500 per post per day for 4 to 5 days. This is a starting point, not a guarantee; adjust once you see results.
5. **Keep the winners:** after 4 to 5 days, look at cost per link click and how many new shops signed up in Admin > Shops. Keep the best 2 posts running and pause the rest.
6. **Answer fast:** reply to comments and messages within the hour, and share your WhatsApp number (070 642 1998). Fast replies turn clicks into sign-ups.
7. **Check the Sinhala text** with a native speaker before boosting, especially any words you would say differently in your area.
