# Sidadiya: 10 feature cards (captions)

Cards are 1080 x 1350 (Instagram / Facebook portrait). Screens use demo data from the sample shop "ABC Fashion".

1. **Your own online shop**: A phone-friendly store with your logo, a WhatsApp button and a link you can share anywhere. Buyers browse, search and order in a few taps.
2. **Easy checkout**: Cash on delivery, bank transfer or card. Buyers see everything on one screen and can fix any detail before they place the order.
3. **Live order tracking**: Every buyer gets a tracking page that updates by itself: placed, confirmed, packed, shipped, delivered. Far fewer "where is my parcel?" messages.
4. **Receipts sent for you**: We send a clean digital receipt on your behalf. Buyers can print it or save it as a PDF, and the link works for 30 days.
5. **Emails in your shop's name**: Order placed, order confirmed and shipped emails go out automatically in your shop's name, with the courier and tracking number.
6. **Your shop in your pocket**: Sales, new orders and your next steps in one place. Run the whole shop from your phone.
7. **Receipt on WhatsApp in one tap**: Send the buyer their receipt link on WhatsApp with one tap. No typing, no screenshots.
8. **Print waybills**: A6 shipping labels with a QR code, the buyer's address and the cash-on-delivery amount. Print one, or a whole day's orders at once.
9. **Know where orders come from** (Pro): See which towns order the most, so you can aim your ads and plan deliveries.
10. **See which app brings buyers** (Pro): WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or Google: see which one brings visitors and orders, so you post where it works.
