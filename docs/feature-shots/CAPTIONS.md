# Sidadiya: all features (captions)

31 cards, 1080 x 1350 (Instagram / Facebook portrait). Screens use demo data from the sample shop "ABC Fashion". Plan labels show where a feature needs Business or Pro.

## Getting started

1. **Open your shop in 3 steps**: Pick a name, colours and a logo, and watch your shop come to life in the live preview as you type. 14 days free, no card needed.
2. **Your own online shop**: A phone-friendly store with your logo, a WhatsApp button and a link you can share anywhere. Buyers browse, search and order in a few taps.
3. **Make it look like you**: Choose a store layout and a colour set. Every colour set is checked so text stays easy to read.
4. **Logo and cover, cropped for you**: Upload any photo and crop it right in the app. No design tools needed.

## Products

5. **Add products in seconds**: Name, price, sale price, photos, and sizes or colours with their own prices and stock.
6. **Stock that watches itself**: Every product shows its stock, and a "Low stock" tag warns you before you run out.
7. **Product pages that sell**: Big photos, price, stock and options on one clean page, with Add to cart always in reach.

## Selling

8. **Easy checkout**: Buyers see everything on one screen and can fix any detail before they place the order.
9. **Get paid your way**: Cash on delivery, bank transfer with your account details, or card. Turn each one on or off.
10. **Card payments with your own OnePay**: Money goes straight to your bank. Your keys are encrypted and never shown again after saving.
11. **Delivery fees your way**: Set your delivery fee, free delivery above an amount, and pickup from the shop.
12. **Coupons and discounts** (Business and Pro): Create codes like WELCOME10 with a minimum order, usage limit and expiry date.

## Orders

13. **Every order in one place**: New, confirmed, shipped and delivered orders in tabs. Search by name or order number.
14. **Print waybills**: A6 shipping labels with a QR code, the buyer's address and the cash-on-delivery amount. Print one, or a whole day's orders at once.
15. **Mark shipped with tracking**: Add the courier and tracking number. The buyer gets an email and sees it on their tracking page.
16. **Receipt on WhatsApp in one tap**: Send the buyer their receipt link on WhatsApp with one tap. No typing, no screenshots.

## For your buyers

17. **Emails in your shop's name**: Order placed, confirmed and shipped emails go out automatically in your shop's name, with the courier and tracking number.
18. **Live order tracking**: A tracking page that updates by itself: placed, confirmed, packed, shipped, delivered. Far fewer "where is my parcel?" messages.
19. **Receipts sent for you**: We send a clean digital receipt on your behalf. Buyers can print it or save it as a PDF, and the link works for 30 days.
20. **Buyers confirm delivery**: One tap on the tracking page tells you the parcel arrived safely.
21. **Honest reviews from real buyers**: Only buyers who received their order can rate it, so every review is real.
22. **Stars that build trust**: Ratings show on your store and product pages, so new buyers order with confidence.

## Growth

23. **Your shop in your pocket**: Sales, new orders and your next steps in one place. Run the whole shop from your phone.
24. **Sales reports** (Business and Pro): Revenue, orders, average order and a sales chart for any date range.
25. **Know where orders come from** (Pro): See which towns order the most, so you can aim your ads and plan deliveries.
26. **See which app brings buyers** (Pro): WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or Google: see which one brings visitors and orders.
27. **Weekly sales email**: Every Monday: last week's sales, orders, top products and where visitors came from.

## Trust

28. **Ready-made policy pages**: Refund, return, privacy and terms pages written for you. Edit and publish in minutes.
29. **Verified business badge**: Get the Verified Sri Lankan Business seal on your store. Your documents stay private.
30. **Staff accounts** (Pro): Give your team their own login and choose exactly what each person can see.
31. **Simple plans, free to start**: 14 days free. Clear monthly prices in rupees, and a bar that shows how many days are left.
